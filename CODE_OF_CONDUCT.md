# Code of conduct

gpucanvas follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

In short:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Don't publish anyone's private information without their permission.
- Accept that a maintainer may close, edit or remove contributions that break these rules.

## Reporting

Email **contact@berkant.me** if someone breaks these rules or makes you feel unwelcome. Reports
are read by the maintainer and kept private. You will get a reply, and the steps taken follow the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent
ban, depending on what happened.
